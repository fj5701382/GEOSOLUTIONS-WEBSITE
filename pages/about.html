Committed to academic excellence since 2012

Geosolution Educational Services is an education, technology and consultancy company based on Ikorodu Road, Lagos.

Our story

From JAMB coaching to a full education centre

Geosolution commenced operations on 22 October 2012, founded by educators who saw the gap in quality, affordable exam preparation in Lagos.

We have since grown from JAMB coaching into WAEC and NECO preparation, IELTS, admission processing and GeoTech Academy — our digital skills arm. Today we have trained 6,000+ students and 300+ tech graduates.
