Admission Processing

End-to-end admission support — from JAMB to departmental screening

Universities, polytechnics & colleges Ikorodu Road, Lagos
Register Now View Pricing

Course Description

End-to-end support for university, polytechnic and college of education admission processes — from JAMB to departmental screening.

Our consultants handle the paperwork and deadlines, and give you honest guidance on courses and institutions, so you can focus on preparing for your exams.

Services Covered

  • JAMB change of institution
  • O'Level result upload & verification
  • Direct entry applications
  • Post-UTME preparation
  • Career path counselling
  • Scholarship research & applications
  • Study abroad guidance
  • CV & personal statement writing

What You'll Get

  • One point of contact for your admission process
  • Guidance on course and institution choices
  • Help meeting portal deadlines
  • Post-UTME preparation support

Office Hours

Visit our centre during office hours (Monday – Friday, 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM; Saturday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM) or start on WhatsApp.

FAQs

Which institutions do you help with?

We support admission into universities, polytechnics and colleges of education.

What documents should I bring?

Bring your JAMB details and O'Level results (or result slips). Our team will tell you if anything else is needed for your application.

How do I register?

Click Register Now, complete the short online form and save your reference number. You'll use it to make payment and to access the student portal.
