About this course

Structured IELTS and TOEFL preparation for students targeting international universities in the UK, Canada, USA, Australia and beyond.

Coaching is built around the band score you need. You'll practise every module, sit full-length mock tests, and get guidance on registering for your exam with the British Council.

Modules Covered

  • Listening
  • Reading
  • Writing
  • Speaking
  • Full-length mock tests
  • TOEFL preparation

What you'll get

  • Band-targeted coaching
  • Mock full-length test sessions
  • Feedback on writing and speaking
  • British Council exam registration guidance
  • Study-abroad advice from our consultancy team

Class schedule

Morning, afternoon and weekend classes are available to fit around school and work. Choose your preferred schedule when you register, and our team will confirm your class time.

Questions

When does the next batch start?

New IELTS classes open regularly. Chat with us on WhatsApp or call +234 701 467 3935 and we'll give you the next start date and available class times.

Can I join mid-session?

Speak to our admissions team first — they'll check space in your preferred class and advise how to catch up on anything you've missed.

Will you help me register for the exam?

Yes. We provide guidance on registering for the IELTS exam with the British Council.

Can you help with my study-abroad application?

Yes. Our Educational Consultancy service covers study-abroad guidance, scholarship research and applications, and CV & personal statement writing.

How do I register?

Click Register Now, complete the short online form and save your reference number. You'll use it to make payment and to access the student portal.
