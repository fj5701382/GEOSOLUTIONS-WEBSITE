Course Description

Intensive coaching for the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination with computer-based practice, past questions and subject mastery sessions.

Because the UTME is written on computer, students practise under real exam conditions in our CBT lab so exam day feels familiar. Our JOT (JAMB Orientation Training) sessions add time-management techniques, past-question pattern analysis and course-selection advice.

Subjects Covered

  • Use of English
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • Economics
  • Government
  • Literature in English

What You'll Get

  • Tutors with 5+ years of exam-prep experience
  • Daily mock CBT practice tests

  • Results tracking & feedback
  • Career guidance and course selection advice

Class Schedule

Morning, afternoon and weekend classes are available to fit around school and work. Choose your preferred schedule when you register, and our team will confirm your class time.

FAQs

When does the next batch start?

New JAMB classes open regularly. Chat with us on WhatsApp or call +234 701 467 3935 and we'll give you the next start date and available class times.

Can I join mid-session?

Speak to our admissions team first — they'll check space in your preferred class and advise how to catch up on anything you've missed.

Do you also help with JAMB admission issues?

Yes. Our Admission Processing service covers JAMB change of institution, O'Level result upload and verification, direct entry applications and Post-UTME preparation.

How do I register?

Click Register Now, complete the short online form and save your reference number. You'll use it to make payment and to access the student portal.
