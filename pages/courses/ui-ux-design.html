UI/UX Design

Design beautiful, user-friendly digital products

3 Months Beginner

Course Description

Design beautiful, user-friendly apps and websites. Learn Figma, wireframing, prototyping and user research techniques.

You'll design real screens and flows, test them with users and build a design portfolio.

What You'll Learn

  • Figma
  • Wireframing
  • Prototyping
  • User research
  • Design systems
  • Portfolio projects

What You'll Get

  • Practical, project-based learning designed for real-world employment
  • Hands-on projects with expert instructors and peer support
  • A portfolio of work to show employers and clients
  • A certificate employers recognise
  • Flexible payment plans

Class Schedule

Classes run on weekdays and weekends. Choose a morning, afternoon or weekend schedule when you register, and our team will confirm your class time.

FAQs

Do I need to be good at drawing?

No. UI/UX design is about solving problems for users; the course starts at beginner level.

When does the next batch start?

New UI/UX Design classes open regularly. Chat with us on WhatsApp or call +234 701 467 3935 and we'll give you the next start date and available class times.

Can I pay in instalments?

Flexible payment plans are available — ask our team for the options.

How do I register?

Click Register Now, complete the short online form and save your reference number. You'll use it to make payment and to access the student portal.
